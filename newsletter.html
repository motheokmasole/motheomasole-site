Who it’s for

For people living a little against the curve.

If you’re trying to live with purpose and actually act on it while everyone around you drifts along, this is a small weekly reminder that there are other people doing it with you.

What it is

A journal that happens to be public.

Each issue is one reflection on something real from my week, whether that’s an ordinary moment, an observation or a small shift in thinking, and I work through it on the page as it happened.

It reads like a journal entry I decided to send, so you get the actual train of thought and you can take from it whatever is useful to you.

  • One honest reflection each week.
  • Under a minute to read, which is the whole promise and it’s in the name.
  • Written from what’s actually going on in my life that week.
  • For people who want to live with intention.
