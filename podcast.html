Why this exists

Most people our age have the ambition. What we rarely get is access.

Access to the right conversations, the right role models and the right kind of honesty about what it takes to live with purpose when it’s so much easier to just stay busy.

With Intention puts those conversations out in the open, so you can hear them without needing to know the right people first.

Take your purpose back into your own hands.

What to expect

Older brother, not expert.

01

Conversations with people who’ve found clarity

I sit down with people who’ve found real clarity in their own work and ask them what actually helped them get there.

02

Solo episodes, thinking out loud

Between the interviews I record reflective episodes where I work through whatever I’m wrestling with myself, as someone who is a few steps ahead and still very much on the way.

03

The questions that actually matter

Purpose, identity and ambition. What to do when the path isn’t obvious, how to want things for the right reasons, and how to own your meaning so you can stop waiting for it.
