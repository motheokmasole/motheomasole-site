Privacy and cookies

The short version is that I only collect what I need to email you, I never sell it, and you can leave at any time.

Who is responsible for your details

This site is run by Motheo Masole, an individual based in Bath, United Kingdom. I am the data controller for anything you share here. You can reach me about any of this at motheo@motheomasole.com.

What I collect and why

  • Newsletter and community waitlist forms. Your first name (optional) and your email address, so I can send you WILIOM Wednesdays and, if you joined the waitlist, updates about the Intentional High Achievers community. I rely on your consent for this, which you give when you submit the form.
  • Emails you send me. If you email me, I keep the message so I can reply to you.
  • Basic technical logs. The company that hosts this site keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

I don’t collect anything else, and this site doesn’t use analytics or advertising trackers.

Who handles your details for me

  • Kit (kit.com) stores the email list and sends the emails. Kit records whether emails are opened and which links are clicked.
  • Netlify (netlify.com) hosts the site and passes form submissions to Kit.

Both companies are based in the United States, so your details are transferred there under the safeguards those companies provide for UK data. I never sell or rent your details to anyone.

How long I keep them

I keep your details for as long as you’re subscribed. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask me to delete your details completely at any time.

Cookies

This site doesn’t set any cookies of its own, which is why you don’t see a cookie banner.

The podcast page can show a Spotify player. It only loads when you press the button to load it, and from that point Spotify may set its own cookies and collect data under Spotify’s privacy policy. You can always open the show in Spotify directly if you’d prefer.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the details I hold about you, have them corrected or deleted, object to how they’re used, or withdraw your consent. Email me and I’ll sort it out within a month.

If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If I change how I use your details, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
