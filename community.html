Intentional High Achievers · A free community

Good grades were never the whole point.

A free community for students who want more than the grade. People building a life they actually enjoy, with a system they choose on purpose every day. Join the waitlist and you’ll be the first in when the doors open.

Free. Small on purpose.

By joining you agree to receive emails about the community and my weekly letter, WILIOM Wednesdays, and you can unsubscribe at any time. How your details are used.

Who it’s for

For students who want the grade and the life.

You’re ambitious, but you’ve started asking why. Why this degree, why that internship, why the version of success everyone around you is chasing. This is for students who want to answer those questions on purpose, alongside people asking the same ones.

What’s inside

Small, active, and built together.

  • A short Friday call to reflect on the week and set up the next one, with people on the same path.
  • Free early access to the tools I’m building, so you can shape them before anyone else sees them.
  • Resources and frameworks for building a system you actually enjoy, not one you survive.
  • Real conversations with students who want more out of university than a grade and a job title.
The right people, not the most people

We’re keeping it small so everyone actually talks, learns and helps each other.
